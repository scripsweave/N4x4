Thanks for the kind words and both suggestions!

I've added an optional voice picker for the next update. Under Settings → Audio,
with Voice Prompts selected, you'll be able to choose an installed Apple voice
and tap Preview Voice to hear it. The current voice stays the default, and the
cues remain in English. For more natural-sounding options, you can download
Apple's Enhanced or Premium English voices in iPhone Settings → Accessibility →
Read & Speak → Voices → English, then return to N4x4 to select one. On older iOS,
Read & Speak is called Spoken Content.

I've also added the extra interval button. It's hidden by default: turn on
Settings → Intervals & Durations → Show Bonus Round Button. During your final
work interval, tap +1 Bonus Round to add recovery and one more work interval
before cooldown, just for that workout. You can undo it before the added
recovery starts. The option works on iPhone and Apple Watch.
