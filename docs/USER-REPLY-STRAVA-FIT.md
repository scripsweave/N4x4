Hi,

Thanks for the kind words and for raising this.

I checked the Strava limitation: its Apple Health import only accepts workouts
recorded with Apple's own Workout app, so it won't import N4x4 sessions through
that connection.

I've added FIT activity export for the next update. Once it's available, open a
saved workout in N4x4 History, tap Share → Export Activity (FIT), save the file,
and upload it through Strava's website using Upload Activity → File. The export
includes the recorded timing and intervals, plus heart rate and active calories
when available. Automatic Strava sync isn't included in this update.

I've also improved the default naming to include Norwegian 4×4 and the activity,
such as “Norwegian 4×4 — Treadmill”, and added editable workout names in N4x4.
Apple Fitness can still show its standard activity category, and you can rename
the activity in Strava after importing.

Thanks for helping make the app better!
Jan
