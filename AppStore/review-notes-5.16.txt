N4x4 is an interval-training timer with spoken workout coaching and live heart-rate feedback. No account or login is required.

New in 5.16 (optional appearance; no new permissions or background modes):
Open Settings > Display > Appearance and select Fall Colors. Home and workout accents become warm autumn colors. The selection persists until changed back to Default; no date-based switching occurs. The phone sends the choice to the paired Watch and Live Activity. Heart-rate zone status colors and workout behavior are unchanged.
Physical iPhone/Watch hardware is required for live heart rate; the Simulator cannot produce it.

Background Bluetooth (Guideline 2.5.4):
1. Wear a standard Bluetooth Low Energy heart-rate monitor, or enable Broadcast Heart Rate on a compatible Garmin/WHOOP device.
2. Open Settings > Health & Devices > Heart Rate Monitor. Start discovery and select the monitor. Discovery is user initiated; launching the app or opening settings does not scan or request Bluetooth permission.
3. Tap START. Current BPM appears in the workout view. Return to the iPhone Home Screen, then reopen N4x4: the monitor continues delivering readings during the workout. This uses Core Bluetooth's Heart Rate Service and HR Measurement characteristic.

Background audio (Guideline 2.5.4):
1. Select Voice Prompts under Settings > Audio > Audio Cues. Enable Halfway Prompts and 10-Second Warnings, with audible media volume.
2. Set short intervals under Settings > Intervals & Durations.
3. Tap START, hear the phase announcement, then return to the iPhone Home Screen while the workout continues. Halfway, ten-second and interval coaching remains audible in the background or under screen lock.
4. Pause/finish ends workout background audio. Silent mode does not provide spoken cues.

The required background modes support these workout features, not advertisements, downloads or unrelated work.
