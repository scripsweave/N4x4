N4x4 is an interval-training timer with spoken workout coaching and live heart-rate feedback. No account or login is required.

New in 5.13:
1. FIT export: complete a workout or open one in History, tap Share > Export Activity (FIT). Save through the native share sheet and manually upload to a compatible service. No Strava login or native account sync is included. JSON export remains available. Legacy workouts without reliable start/end timing retain JSON export.
2. Workout names: edit the workout name in the saved summary or History. New Health exports use the chosen activity and protocol name; already-saved Health workouts are not replaced or renamed.
3. Optional bonus round: enable Settings > Intervals & Durations > Show Bonus Round Button (off by default). During the last work interval, tap +1 Bonus Round to add recovery and one work interval before cooldown. Undo is available until the added recovery starts. Phone and Watch support this option.
4. Voice picker: Settings > Audio, select Voice Prompts, then Voice. Choose an installed Apple English voice and use Preview Voice between workouts. The existing default is unchanged. Additional Enhanced/Premium Apple voices are installed through iPhone Accessibility settings. No Personal Voice permission is requested.
5. Watch calories: Settings > Health & Devices > Review Health Access requests Workouts, Heart Rate and Active Energy access. New Watch-recorded workouts can attach original Apple Watch Active Energy samples to the single phone-saved Health workout, without creating duplicate energy samples. Health and Watch synchronization can delay the save; History displays the pending state and offers Save Without Calories. Phone-only sessions do not invent calories. Existing Health workouts are not backfilled. Physical iPhone/Watch hardware is required for live HR and energy.

Background Bluetooth (Guideline 2.5.4):
1. Wear a standard Bluetooth Low Energy heart-rate monitor, or enable Broadcast Heart Rate on a compatible Garmin/WHOOP device.
2. Open Settings > Health & Devices > Heart Rate Monitor. Start discovery and select the monitor. Discovery is user initiated; launching the app or opening settings does not scan or request Bluetooth permission.
3. Tap START. Current BPM appears in the workout view. Return to the iPhone Home Screen, then reopen N4x4: the monitor continues delivering readings during the workout. This uses Core Bluetooth's Heart Rate Service and HR Measurement characteristic.

Background audio (Guideline 2.5.4):
1. Select Voice Prompts under Settings > Audio > Audio Cues. Enable Halfway Prompts and 10-Second Warnings, with audible media volume.
2. Set short intervals under Settings > Intervals & Durations.
3. Tap START, hear the phase announcement, then return to the iPhone Home Screen while the workout continues. Halfway, ten-second and interval coaching remains audible in the background or under screen lock.
4. Pause/finish ends workout background audio. Silent mode does not provide spoken cues.

The required background modes support these workout features, not advertisements, downloads or unrelated work.
