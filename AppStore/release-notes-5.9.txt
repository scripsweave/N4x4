This update fixes workout timing in Apple Health and improves Apple Watch pause and resume.

• Paused time and gaps after recovering an interrupted workout no longer count toward the duration saved to Apple Health.
• Resuming from Apple Watch continues the remaining interval without jumping ahead or ending the workout early.
• Additional checks prevent inconsistent older workout records from being exported with an inflated duration.

These fixes apply to future saves. Workouts already saved to Apple Health are not changed automatically.
